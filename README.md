# Camilia University

The Camilia University reception. Push open the doors of a lecture hall, choose a chair, and the menu is written on the board.

## Using the hall

1. Open the doors, or enter directly.
2. Click a chair. The arrow keys move the selection, and Enter confirms it.
3. Once seated, click a title on the board. Keys 1 to 6 open the chapters. Escape steps back, then stands you up. Standing in the hall, Back to the hallway (or Escape) fades to black and returns you outside the doors.

The chapters: courses, campus, admissions, research, student life, and the office.

Right of the double doors, a smaller door leads to a bedroom with blue walls: a bed under the window, a desk with a computer, a wardrobe and a standing mirror. Open it by clicking the door or the Bedroom link. Move the mouse (or drag on a phone, or use the arrow keys) to look around, and press Escape or Back to the hallway to leave.

### The wardrobe and the mirror

In the bedroom, click (or tap) the wardrobe. Its doors swing open and you step in front of a standing mirror, where your character appears. The first time, choose a base body: a slim man or a slim woman. Each starts in a plain, casual outfit (grey T-shirt, dark straight jeans and white sneakers for him; white T-shirt, straight jeans and white sneakers for her).

The wardrobe panel (on the right, or at the bottom on a phone) has two tabs:

- Body: switch between the two base bodies, adjust height, build, shoulders and hips, and pick a skin tone, a hairstyle and a hair colour.
- Clothes: a top, a bottom, shoes and outerwear, each with several cuts and colours. Underwear only shows the base body: a smooth, mannequin-like figure in plain underwear (briefs, plus a simple bra for the woman), with no anatomical detail.

Every change shows in the mirror straight away. Drag on the scene, use the turn buttons, or press the arrow keys to turn the character. Reset to base outfit restores the starting clothes. Done, Back to the room, or Escape closes the wardrobe. The character is saved in this browser.

Recorded sounds follow those motions: the latch and the double doors, footsteps on stone then carpet, the wooden tiers, the chair, and the chalk. The hall adds its own reverb. The button at the top right mutes them, and the choice is remembered in this browser. Sources and licenses are in `public/sounds/CREDITS.md`.
